# TALA achievements — 78 badges

Newly earned badges show a non-blocking golden notification and an offline unlock chime. Multiple unlocks queue one at a time (3.2 seconds each); close dismisses the queue. Replays, restored progress and app startup do not re-announce existing badges. The chime respects SFX volume/mute; motion respects device and in-app reduced-motion settings. Switching learner, opening teacher controls, resetting progress or hiding the app clears pending celebrations. Badges are earned only after a successful saved completion, not by browsing their page.

Badges are computed from the active learner’s saved progress; no extra recording, account, or online service is needed. Existing learners receive qualifying badges automatically. Resetting that learner’s progress also resets their badges. Audio sliders are device-wide; voiceover volume is unchanged.

Completion badges accept any star rating. Only the explicitly five-star badges require five stars. These are motivational rewards, not mastery or curriculum assessments. Replaying a stage does not increase the distinct-stage count; duplicate completed IDs are ignored.
